EVIDENCE BEFORE SERVICE

Feasibility & risks

Restoration and engineering are prerequisites—not assumptions to skip.

Critical due diligence
Cooum and Adyar possibilities are restoration-dependent. No proposed corridor should be interpreted as navigable. Assess water quality, ecology, flood function, public safety and restoration progress before any passenger service study.
